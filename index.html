The Process

How a name gets into the brief.

Nothing is added on gut feel. A name has to clear the screen, show a public catalyst, and carry both sides of the argument before it goes out.

01

The screen runs before 9:00 AM ET

Identifies US-listed stocks with notable pre-market movement and a confirmed public catalyst such as earnings, guidance, an FDA decision, or major company news. No confirmed catalyst: the idea does not advance.

02

Both sides are written up

The upside argument and the downside risk are written for every name. No idea publishes with only one side. The risk case is mandatory, not optional.

03

Published at 9:00 AM ET

The brief is in the group at 9:00 AM ET, 30 minutes before the NYSE opens at 9:30. Ticker, catalyst, both sides, key risk.

04

Every idea goes on the public research log

Reference price at publication, subsequent price, and S&P 500 comparison. Positive and negative outcomes stay on record.
